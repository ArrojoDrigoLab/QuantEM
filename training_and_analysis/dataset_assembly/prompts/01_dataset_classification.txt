Optional tag groups are allowed when they improve filtering. For cell lines,

`published_data` must be `Yes` for every catalog item because this repository is

When a paper has multiple public deposits for related data, choose the

`download_or_manifest_urls`; keep secondary deposits, analysis bundles,
