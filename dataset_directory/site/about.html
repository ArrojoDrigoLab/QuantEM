About this directory

A browsable catalog of the electron microscopy corpus assembled for QuantEM — what is in it, where each dataset lives, and what it looks like.

The corpus

Loading current totals…

The corpus was assembled from three streams: public repositories searched through their APIs and triaged for intracellular biological EM; a campaign contacting the corresponding authors of published EM studies whose underlying images had never been deposited; and previously unpublished acquisitions from our own group and collaborators. A substantial share of it is newly released to the community through this work.
